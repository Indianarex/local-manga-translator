Automatic manga translation → English

Paste the address of a chapter reading page above. The pages are fetched, then the text in every speech bubble is detected, read and translated into English, page by page, entirely on your computer, with no API key and no online service. The next pages are translated ahead of time so you can keep reading without waiting.

Source languages: Japanese, Chinese (simplified and traditional), Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian. They are detected automatically, or you can pick one in the toolbar.

The first time a language is used, the models it needs are downloaded once into the models folder (1 to 6 GB).
